# super secret samples

**[WORK IN PROGRESS]** _super secret samples_ is a music samples library tailored to professional music producers seeking unique, high-quality samples. The samples are guaranteed royalty-free and homemade with care.

![super secret samples homepage](https://valentinorrit.com/img/projects_sss-librarypage.webp?raw=true 'super secret samples homepage')

Users can search for and download samples by music genre, instrument, tempo, key, and various tags. Upon creating an account. Users can also request the creation and upload of samples based on their specific criteria and may obtain exclusive access to these custom samples.
